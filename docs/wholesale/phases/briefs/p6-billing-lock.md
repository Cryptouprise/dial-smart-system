# JOB P6 - Billing lock: Stripe subscription state drives access (lane: ultra, BUILD contract)

- `organizations` has live columns `billing_enabled`, `subscription_tier`, `subscription_status`, `stripe_customer_id`, `trial_ends_at`, `plan_id` (FK plans), `unlimited_access`, `unlimited_reason`, `max_*`. - Rule: a company whose subscription is not in good standing must be locked out of everything except a "paused" page with a link to pay, and its data must become invisible to its users through RLS as well (belt and braces). Platform operator and service_role are never locked.
