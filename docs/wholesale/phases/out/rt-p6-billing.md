### 4. VERDICT

**REDRAFT.**

Reasons: blockers #15/#16/#17 mean the belt-and-braces RLS lock is defeated by the phase-1 company-membership policies that the draft leaves in place — a paused org's members can still read all four core tables. Blocker #2 leaves `phone_numbers` entirely unlocked. Blockers #4/#5/#6 mean the webhook can permanently miss a state transition (claimed-but-failed event, `invoice.paid` not clearing `past_due`, delete persisting `active`). Blockers #10/#11 mean the frontend fails open. The correct shape is a RESTRICTIVE SELECT policy per table (which ANDs with every permissive policy, including phase-1's), claim-after-write idempotency, `invoice.paid` setting `subscription_status = 'active'`, forced `'canceled'` on delete, and a fail-closed frontend. The draft also omits the DESIGN NOTE and TESTS the brief requires (p6-billing-lock.md:19).
