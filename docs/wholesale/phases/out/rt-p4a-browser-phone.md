### 4. VERDICT

**REDRAFT.** The draft cannot be applied as-is: the migration contains its own `BEGIN;`/`COMMIT;` (blocker 1) and a two-argument `user_in_organization` call (blocker 2), both of which fail at `apply_migration`. Beyond apply-time failures, the entire webhook→`call_logs` matching path is broken for both providers (blockers 3–8): Telnyx events are dropped because `telnyx_call_control_id` is never written, and Twilio callbacks are matched against the wrong column. The DNC check is scoped per-user instead of per-company (blocker 10), which is a compliance leak across companies, and the lead lookup blocks calling teammates' leads (blocker 9), regressing the phase-1 company scoping. `twilio-signature.ts` imports from a module not in the material (blocker 11), and `voice-call-events` 503s on a secret not in the brief's list (blocker 12). Fix blockers 1–13 and re-submit; do not apply.
