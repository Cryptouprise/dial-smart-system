### 4. VERDICT

**APPLY-WITH-FIXES (blockers 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 9, 10, 11).**

Blockers 1 and 2 are the most dangerous: the RESTRICTIVE policy as written hides NULL-org rows from their owners (a live regression on 5 leads and 519 call_logs per LIVE-SCHEMA.md:45) and locks out platform operators, contradicting the brief's "never locked" rule. Blockers 3-5 are webhook correctness bugs that can un-pause a canceled org or extend grace on duplicate delivery. Blockers 7, 9, 10 are deploy/lockout risks. Blocker 11 is a one-line grant. The RESTRICTIVE-policy approach itself is sound and arguably better than the brief's literal drop/recreate (it also locks the phase-1 company policies), but the foreman should record that deviation explicitly (gap #1).
