# S6. See what others cannot: contrarian angles, hidden risks, second-order effects

Read 00-common.md (attached) first.

TASK: Think about all angles. Where is the real moat (data, coach learning per team, carrier relationships, compliance posture, speed)?
What breaks at 10x volume (carrier filtering and number burn, 10DLC throughput, answer rates, data costs, AI call regulation, state
licensing waves for wholesaling, buyer fatigue, team quality)? What do most wholesalers and most dialer vendors get wrong? What would a
well-funded competitor do that this team cannot, and what can this team do that a funded competitor cannot? What quiet asymmetries exist
(e.g. the AI coach as a training product, the transcript corpus, the permit data as a solar + roofing + wholesale multi-use asset,
the door-to-door pack as a physical-world edge)? What are the second-order effects of full automation (reputation, legal, relationship
with sellers)? Include at least five angles that argue AGAINST the current plan.

OUTPUT FORMAT (markdown):
1. `## 15 angles` : each: one-line angle | why others miss it | what to do about it in one paragraph | confidence | Label.
2. `## The five against` : the strongest cases against the plan as it stands, each with the cheapest test that would settle it.
3. `## Early warnings` : signals to watch monthly that say an angle is turning real.
4. `## Assumptions`.
Max 2,500 words. Label every claim FACT (path:line) / INDUSTRY (confidence) / IDEA.
