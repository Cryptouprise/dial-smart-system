# Law Firm AI Intake Beta — VSL & Sales Asset Brief

## VSL Objective
The VSL appears **after** the prospect has experienced the personalized legal demo and seen their own missed-opportunity math.

It should not explain AI from scratch.

Its job is to widen the prospect’s mental model from:

> “This is an AI receptionist.”

into:

> “This can manage the entire lead lifecycle around my firm.”

Target length: **60–90 seconds.**

## Core transition
Opening line / screen:

> **That was only one leak.**

Then:

> You just saw what Call Boss can do when somebody calls your firm after hours. But the phone ringing is only the beginning of a lead’s journey.

## Higgsfield Production Direction

### Avatar tone
- Premium operator / strategist, not “tech bro.”
- Confident, calm, slightly provocative.
- Conversational delivery.
- Avoid generic corporate-news-presenter energy.

### Visual style
- Dark premium UI matching Call Boss.
- Indigo / violet / cyan accents for legal beta.
- Animated product screens rather than stock-lawyer footage whenever possible.
- Short flashes of law-office context are fine, but product should remain the hero.

### Editing rhythm
- Hook in first 2 seconds.
- New visual beat every 3–5 seconds.
- Keep avatar on screen only when useful.
- Use UI motion graphics for the economic and lifecycle concepts.
- Captions always on.
- Muted autoplay with clear sound control.

## Post-VSL Close

### Headline
**Ready to put this on your firm?**

### Supporting copy
“Start narrow. Run it against one measurable intake problem. Keep what works. Expand only when the next workflow has a clear job to do.”

### Choice 1 — Start My Beta
Best for prospect who already understands the value.

Do not ask again for:
- firm name,
- website,
- name,
- email,
- phone,
- inbound volume estimates.

Ask only missing launch information.

### Choice 2 — Talk to Someone First
Offer a short implementation / fit conversation.

Position it as:
**15 minutes to confirm routing, intake, and integration requirements.**

### Choice 3 — Show Me Lead Recovery
Opens a second mini-demo / calculator focused on first-party stale lead economics.

## Lead Recovery Mini-Demo Concept

### Opening
**Before you buy another lead, work the ones you already paid for.**

### Inputs
- Approximate old-lead count.
- Average monthly new leads.
- Percent that never book.
- Percent that no-show.
- Percent that consult but never retain.
- Average signed-client value.

### Output
Do not promise a conversion rate.

Show sensitivity cases based on tiny recovery percentages:
- 0.5% recovery.
- 1% recovery.
- 2% recovery.

Why low sensitivity values are compelling:
With already-acquired databases, even small recovery percentages can show meaningful economics without relying on aggressive claims.

### Demonstration behavior
Let prospect experience a database-reactivation call with firm-specific context.

Example roleplay:
“You reached out to [Firm] a while back and I wanted to see whether you ever got the help you were looking for.”

Stay permission-based, conversational, and easy to exit.

## Required Sales Assets

### Asset 1 — Personalized live demo
The highest-value asset. Everything should drive here.

Explains the larger vision after proof.

### Asset 3 — Missed-call calculator
Can exist both inside demo and as standalone content.

Second doorway / upsell.

### Asset 5 — One-page security / legal-safe FAQ
Answer:
- Is this legal advice?
- What happens with client/prospect data?
- Can it transfer to humans?
- Can we control the script?
- Does it record calls?
- Can we configure retention?
- Can it integrate with our CRM / case-management system?

### Asset 6 — Beta one-pager
One problem, one scope, one price, one implementation process.

### Asset 7 — Proof clips
Short anonymized / approved examples showing:
- intake quality,
- routing,
- urgency capture,
- successful handoff,
- reactivation conversations.

### Asset 8 — Expansion map
A simple visual of the full lead lifecycle.

## VSL Acceptance Criteria
- 90 seconds or less unless testing proves longer wins.
- Prospect understands the larger lifecycle by the end.
- Lead Recovery concept is understandable without jargon.
- No unsupported legal or financial claims.
- VSL does not repeat the live demo.
- CTA is visible immediately after video.
- Mobile experience is clean.
- Captions are present.
- Video can be skipped without blocking purchase intent.
