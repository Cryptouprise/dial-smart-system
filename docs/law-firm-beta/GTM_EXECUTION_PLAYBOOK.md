# Call Boss Law Firm Beta — GTM Execution Playbook

_Last updated: 2026-08-16_

## GTM thesis

Do not scale lead generation before proving the firm can monetize the demand it already has.

The market message has two layers:

1. **Immediate wedge:** stop losing high-intent new-client calls because the office is closed, busy, or slow to respond.
2. **Expansion:** recover more value from every lead the firm already paid to generate across response, follow-up, appointment attendance, and reactivation.

The self-selling funnel is the product demonstration and the sales asset.

---

## Launch objective

First 30 days:

- 25 hand-picked firms contacted.
- 10 personalized demos actually experienced.
- 5 serious beta conversations.
- 3 founding beta firms closed.
- 1–2 usable proof stories / anonymized before-vs-after examples.
- Zero paid-traffic scaling until the demo-completion → high-intent CTA path is credible.

These are internal operating targets, not public claims.

## Target-account scoring

Score every prospect before outreach.

### Demand score — 0 to 5

- +2 actively running Google Ads / Local Services Ads / obvious paid media
- +1 multiple lead forms / prominent consultation CTA
- +1 meaningful review volume / visible market presence
- +1 multiple offices or broader service footprint

### Intake-pain score — 0 to 5

- +2 after-hours call reaches voicemail / weak experience
- +1 no visible 24/7 intake solution
- +1 form submission is not acknowledged quickly during a manual test
- +1 website suggests staff-dependent intake / office-hours coverage

Never impersonate a client or submit sensitive fake facts when testing; use a benign business inquiry when appropriate.

### Fit score — 0 to 5

- +2 consumer-facing high-intent practice
- +1 roughly 2–50 attorneys / decision maker accessible
- +1 uses Clio, Lawmatics, Filevine, MyCase, GHL, HubSpot, or another integration-friendly system
- +1 marketing / intake / operations leader identifiable

### Expansion score — 0 to 5

- +2 likely meaningful historical lead database
- +1 consultation / appointment model
- +1 SMS / email nurture would be natural
- +1 marketing spend suggests Speed-to-Lead economics matter

### Priority

- **16–20:** Tier A — personalized demo built before outreach
- **12–15:** Tier B — direct outreach + demo offer
- **8–11:** Tier C — nurture / content / partner channel
- **<8:** do not spend founder time yet

## The outreach principle

Do not sell “AI.”

Lead with a business observation and a piece of proof.

Best founder-led angle:

> **I built an after-hours intake demo around your actual law firm website. You can experience it yourself without booking a call with me.**

This converts the outreach from a claim into an experience.

---

## Outreach sequence — personalized demo

### Touch 1 — Email

Subject options:
- Built an AI intake demo around {{Firm Name}}
- What happens to {{Firm Name}} calls after hours?
- I made your website answer the phone

Body structure:

1. One sentence proving this is specific to the firm.
2. One sentence on the after-hours / intake problem.
3. Explain that the demo was built from their public website context.
4. Direct link into `/law-firms` or a pre-seeded `/demo?mode=legal&url=...` experience.
5. “No meeting required.”

### Touch 2 — LinkedIn / short note, 1–2 days later

> Sent you something unusual — a live AI intake demo built around your firm. The interesting part isn’t the chatbot. It’s the missed-call and lead-recovery economics after you experience it.

### Touch 3 — Manual business-line call

Goal is not to pitch the full system.

> “I built a live after-hours intake demo using your firm’s public website. I’m trying to get it in front of whoever owns intake or marketing. Who’s the right person?”

### Touch 4 — Lead Recovery follow-up

If they engage but do not start beta:

> “The other piece I’d look at before buying more leads is the database you already paid for — unsigned inquiries, no-shows, ‘call me later,’ and prospects nobody has worked in months. That’s the next workflow we’re building for the legal beta.”

### Touch 5 — Breakup / permission close

> “I don’t want to become another vendor chasing you. Want me to leave the personalized demo active for your team, or should I close the loop?”

## “We built your demo already” workflow

For Tier-A accounts:

1. Identify firm and decision maker.
2. Pre-seed the law-firm demo with public website URL.
3. Verify scrape quality; do not place a call.
4. Generate a clean direct link.
5. Send short personalized outreach.
6. Use funnel events to see whether they entered setup, call screen, ROI, VSL, and CTA.
7. Follow up based on actual engagement, not generic sequence timing.

Examples:
- reached ROI but no CTA → address economics / trust / implementation friction.
- selected Lead Recovery → follow up on database size / old lead sources.
- selected Talk → prioritize immediate human follow-up.
- Start Beta → contact as highest priority.

## Conversion event definitions

### Visitor
Reached `/law-firms`.

### Engaged visitor
Submitted website.

### Demo starter
Reached legal setup.

### Product-qualified lead
Reached ROI or post-demo vision.

### High-intent lead
Selected Start Beta / Talk / Lead Recovery.

### Sales-qualified lead
Has valid contact + firm fit + explicit next-step intent.

### Beta customer
Agreed to founding offer and supplied implementation requirements.

This lets us optimize a product-led funnel without pretending every website visitor is a lead.

---

## Founder follow-up SLA

When the funnel creates a high-intent lead:

- Start Beta: target human response inside **5 minutes during waking business hours**.
- Talk to Someone: inside **15 minutes** when practical.
- Lead Recovery: inside **same business day**, with a database-specific discovery question.

The product sells speed-to-lead; our own sales process should demonstrate it.

---

## Beta discovery questions

Do not restart the demo questionnaire.

Ask only what implementation needs:

1. What calls should the AI answer: after-hours, weekends, overflow, all inbound?
2. Which practice areas should it accept / decline / route?
3. What makes a matter urgent?
4. Where should qualified new clients go?
5. How should existing clients be handled?
6. What calendar / CRM / case-management system should receive data?
7. What recording / disclosure rules does the firm require?
8. Who approves scripts and intake rules?

## Lead Recovery Engine — first implementation scope

### Eligible pools

Start with firm-owned first-party inquiries where outreach is legally / contractually permitted and opt-out status is respected.

Candidate segments:
- form fills never reached,
- callers never retained,
- consultation no-shows,
- qualified prospects who did not sign,
- “call me later” records,
- stale open opportunities,
- older inquiries not marked DNC / opted out.

### Do not start with
- purchased cold consumer lists,
- unclear-consent data,
- active clients,
- DNC / opted-out records,
- matters where firm policy forbids automated outreach.

### Suggested beta cadence concept

This must be customized and compliance-reviewed before production, but product design should support:
- voice attempts at varied permitted times,
- context-aware SMS follow-up,
- email information when appropriate,
- callback scheduling,
- hard stop on opt-out / DNC / retained elsewhere / wrong party / closed matter,
- human handoff on positive intent.

### Primary economic metric

Do not optimize for “calls made.”

Optimize for:
- recovered conversations,
- qualified reopened opportunities,
- booked consultations,
- signed clients where attributable,
- cost per recovered opportunity.

## Founding beta offer

Recommended hypothesis is documented in `OFFER_AND_PRICING.md`.

Working launch position:

> **First 10 founding law firms: $995/month, no setup fee, 1,000 included AI conversation minutes.**

Do not publish until live-call COGS and browser QA are complete.

The offer is intentionally narrow:
- after-hours / weekends / overflow,
- firm-specific intake,
- month-to-month during beta,
- measurable success criteria.

Expansion workflows are sold after the initial product proves itself.

## Paid traffic gate

Do not launch meaningful paid spend until all are true:

- at least 20 qualified visitors have submitted websites,
- at least 10 have completed the personalized demo path,
- at least 3 high-intent CTAs have been generated,
- at least 1–3 firms have agreed to a beta / assisted implementation,
- no critical demo-call context leakage,
- mobile funnel works cleanly,
- follow-up SLA is operational.

When those are true, start with **one channel**, not four.

### Recommended first paid test

Google Search intent around:
- AI receptionist for law firms
- after-hours answering service for law firms
- legal intake answering service
- law firm intake automation
- 24/7 law firm receptionist

Land traffic on `/law-firms`.

Do not send legal-intent traffic to the outbound dialer homepage.

### Initial paid-test rule

Use a deliberately small budget until 100+ qualified landing sessions provide signal.

Optimize first for:
1. website submitted,
2. demo completion,
3. high-intent CTA,
4. beta lead,

not cheap clicks.

## Content engine

Build content around the economic thesis rather than generic “AI is the future” posts.

### Pillar 1 — Intake leakage
- What happens to your law firm’s calls after 5 PM?
- Why more leads do not fix weak intake.
- The hidden cost of voicemail.

### Pillar 2 — Lead economics
- Before you buy another lead, work the ones you already paid for.
- Cost per lead vs. cost per retained client.
- Where qualified inquiries become non-productive.

### Pillar 3 — Speed
- What five-minute response actually looks like operationally.
- Why form submissions should trigger an immediate multichannel workflow.

### Pillar 4 — Practical AI trust
- What an AI intake agent should never do.
- How to keep legal advice out of the receptionist layer.
- Human escalation / approval / auditability.

Every piece should point to the personalized demo, not a generic “book a sales call.”

## Core decision rule

**Do not buy your way around a conversion problem.**

If qualified firms experience a firm-specific call, see their own intake economics, and still do not want a next step, the problem is product / offer / trust / proof — not traffic volume.

Fix that before scaling acquisition.
