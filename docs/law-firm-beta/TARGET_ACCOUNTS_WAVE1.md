# Law Firm AI Intake Beta — Wave 1 Target Accounts

_Last updated: 2026-08-16_

## Purpose

This is a **research / audit list**, not a claim that any firm has poor intake.

Public business listings, websites, and posted hours can tell us where to investigate. They cannot tell us whether a firm has excellent live intake, an answering service, AI, outsourced coverage, or voicemail behind a “24/7” listing.

Before outreach, each Tier-A account should receive a short, benign intake-experience audit and website review. Do not impersonate an injured consumer or submit sensitive fake case details.

### Audit labels

- **Office-hours gap:** public listing shows non-24/7 office availability. Highest-priority wedge candidate.
- **24/7 claim:** public listing advertises 24-hour availability. Audit what the caller actually experiences and position around intake quality / conversion / Lead Recovery rather than assuming missed calls.
- **High demand signal:** strong public visibility / review footprint / multi-market presence suggests meaningful inbound demand.

## Pre-outreach audit checklist

For each Tier-A firm:

- [ ] Confirm current website URL.
- [ ] Identify managing partner / intake director / marketing director / operations contact.
- [ ] Note practice areas.
- [ ] Note number of offices and approximate attorney count.
- [ ] Note visible PPC / LSA / paid-media signals.
- [ ] Identify current contact methods: phone, form, live chat, text, scheduler.
- [ ] Check public business hours.
- [ ] If appropriate, place a benign business-hours / after-hours call to determine routing experience without pretending to have a legal matter.
- [ ] Note whether caller reaches human, AI, answering service, IVR, or voicemail.
- [ ] Note whether form produces immediate acknowledgment / callback during a benign test where permitted.
- [ ] Run firm URL through Call Boss scraper and inspect extracted context.
- [ ] Create direct legal-demo URL.
- [ ] Assign Demand / Intake Pain / Fit / Expansion score from `GTM_EXECUTION_PLAYBOOK.md`.

## Important GTM lesson from this first dataset

A large share of visible PI firms already claim 24/7 availability.

That means the winning category is **not** simply “we answer at night.”

The sales conversation must distinguish:

1. **Availability** — something answers.
2. **Intake quality** — the caller is understood, qualified, and routed correctly.
3. **Conversion system** — qualified opportunities get persistent follow-up.
4. **Recovery system** — old opportunities are systematically reworked instead of abandoned.
5. **Economics** — the firm can see how acquisition spend moves through the lifecycle.

That broader lifecycle story is Call Boss’s strategic advantage over a commodity answering-service comparison.
